A one-day world news poster for make.fun's News image bounty (25 November 2026, $100 in USDC).

`main.py` (pure Python standard library, no network) draws a 1024×1024 newspaper poster — masthead, lead story (Trump tariff threat sinks world markets), a market bar panel, a war-at-sea strip, and 32 dated stories from 7 regions plus Also today — and writes it to `output/entry.png`.

Deterministic: repeated runs write byte-identical PNGs.
